How to Use This Tool

This interactive OSCE practice suite is designed to simulate real exam conditions. Follow the steps below to get the most out of your practice.

1. Getting Started

This tool works best in Google Chrome or Microsoft Edge. Progress is saved in your browser's sessionStorage, meaning it will persist as long as the tab remains open. Closing the tab will reset your progress.

2. Choosing a Station

Navigate to the Cases tab. You can filter stations by Year (All / 2024 / 2023) and Status (All / MATCHED / RELATED / NOT FOUND). There is no system filter—stations are intended to be practised in a random, exam-like manner.

3. Working Through a Station

Each station has five sequential steps: History, Examination, Investigations, Diagnosis, and Management.

  1. Read the station brief and start the 8-minute countdown timer.
  2. Select your answers for each step. Click Check Answer for immediate feedback.
  3. Correct answers are highlighted in green, incorrect in red.
  4. High-yield phrases appear highlighted in feedback text.
  5. Use Next Step to proceed and Finish Station at the end.

4. Audio Practice (Two-Way)

The audio panel provides Examiner prompts and Candidate answer practice:

  • Examiner: Brief — reads the station scenario.
  • Examiner: Current Question — reads the active step's question.
  • Read Feedback — reads the teaching points aloud.
  • Practice Full Presentation — reads the complete Model Answer.
  • Speak Answer — activates speech recognition (STT) to transcribe your verbal answer.
  • Stop — stops any ongoing speech or recognition.

Speech recognition and synthesis work best in Chrome/Edge. If unsupported, text input remains available.

5. Finishing a Station

After completing all five steps, the Model Answer appears. This is a structured, examiner-style presentation you can read and hear via text-to-speech. Use it to refine your verbal technique.

6. Other Sections

  • Mock Exam — 8 random stations in one sitting, with a 60-minute global timer and per-station 8-minute timers. No feedback until the end.
  • Flashcards — 30 curated high-yield cards across specialties. Filter by category or shuffle.
  • OSCE Tips — strategy content including WIPE, SOCRATES, time management, red flags, and more.
  • Progress — visual tracker and reset option.

7. Tips for Effective Practice

  • Time yourself — aim to finish within the 8-minute limit.
  • Speak aloud — verbalise your history, examination, and management.
  • Review Model Answers — compare your phrasing and completeness.
  • Practise across systems — real OSCE stations are random; avoid focusing on one area.
